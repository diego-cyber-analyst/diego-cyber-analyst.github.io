After more than 20 years working in technology and information security, I decided to change the course of my life. I left a stable job and moved to Canada — with nothing lined up on the other side, starting again almost from scratch.

It was a decision that brought together a long-held desire to live abroad and the right moment to finally act on it. And it came with challenges I knew existed on paper, but that feel very different once you're actually living them: the language in everyday life, the financial uncertainty of starting over, and the full weight of an immigration process.

I created this blog to document that journey — the technical side, with procedures and lessons from my day-to-day work implementing and managing tools and infrastructure, and also the human side of moving to a new country and learning to live in another language.

If you're thinking about taking a similar path, or you're simply curious about what making this kind of leap is like, I hope you find something useful here. Welcome.
