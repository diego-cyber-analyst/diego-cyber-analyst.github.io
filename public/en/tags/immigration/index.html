The arrival

Before any suitcase, before the plane, before the Canadian cold, there was a different kind of cold — the knot in my stomach on the day I quit my job. I had a stable job. Good salary, investments, money saved. On paper, everything people tell you not to walk away from. The severance was actually decent — even after losing part of it for resigning — and for a moment I felt a flash of joy. But it came bundled with the math I was already running in my head: that money was going to shrink, and I had nothing lined up on the other side. ...

Why this blog exists

I left a stable job and moved to Canada — with nothing lined up on the other side, starting again almost from scratch. It was a decision that brought together a long-held desire to live abroad and the right moment to finally act on it.
